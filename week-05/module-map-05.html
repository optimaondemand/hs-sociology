❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What do you see once you stop taking your own household for granted?
Berger says sociology begins with a chosen habit of seeing through surfaces. Simmel shows what that vantage point looks like when a person simply occupies it, without choosing to. This week turns both toward the oldest and most familiar institution there is: the family.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS COURTESY
Aristotle calls the household the first school of the virtues, the place where a person first learns to give and receive care, to exercise and submit to authority, and to honor obligations to people who did not choose them and whom they did not choose either. Courtesy, in that light, is not a set of table manners. It is the practiced habit of treating the people closest to you, the ones whose faults you know best, with the same consideration you would extend to a stranger you were still trying to impress.
