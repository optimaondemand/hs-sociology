⚡ TRY IT · CLASS, STATUS, PARTY
Weber says a person's position in society has three separate dimensions. Move each one and watch what happens.
Marx measured stratification on a single axis: whether you own the means of production. Weber's claim is that this is one axis of three, and that the three can pull apart. Try to build a person the single-axis model cannot describe.
Class
Your economic position. What you own, what you can sell, and what the market will give you for it. This is the axis Marx measured.
Owns nothing but labourOwns productive property
Status
Social honour. How much esteem your way of life commands from others, and who will associate with you as an equal. Money can buy goods; it cannot directly buy this.
Held in low regardHighly honoured
Party
Organised power. Your capacity to act with others to make things happen: through a union, a party, a lobby, a church body, a professional association.
Acts aloneCommands an organisation
