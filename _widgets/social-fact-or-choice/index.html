⚡ TRY IT · SOCIAL FACT OR PERSONAL CHOICE?
Eight behaviours. Decide which are social facts and which are genuinely personal choices.
Tap a behaviour to select it, then tap a bin. Some of these feel like choices and are not. Those are the ones worth getting wrong.
Test each one against Durkheim's three criteria from Rules of Sociological Method I. A social fact is external (it existed before you and you did not invent it), general (it holds across the group, not just for you), and constraining (it pushes back when you act against it). All three must hold.
