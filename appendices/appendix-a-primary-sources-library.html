APPENDIX A
Primary Sources Library
Every primary source assigned in this course, in one place, with the one fact that matters most: where you actually find the words.

Every reading is on a course page. Most are reproduced in full on the primary source guide, because the work and its translation are in the public domain.

Where the only English translation is still under copyright, the page is a Text Study: it explains the work section by section in Optima's own words. No week needs a book.
