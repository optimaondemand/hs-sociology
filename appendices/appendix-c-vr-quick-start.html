APPENDIX C
Virtual Reality Quick-Start Guide
Everything a new student needs to complete a solo VR activity in this course. Come back to it whenever a VR term stops you.
❓ WHAT IS THE VR PLATFORM?

Optima Academy runs its VR-based learning through a single virtual reality platform, accessed through a VR headset if you have one, or through a desktop version if you do not. The desktop version costs nothing and runs on most computers made in the last five years.

Inside the platform, you join a session: a shared space where you and, depending on the activity, other students or your facilitator are present together. Every VR activity in this course tells you which session to join and gives you a link to it from the Canvas module.

🚀 GETTING STARTED
  1. Install the VR platform. Download it from your school's approved application portal, or from the platform's official site if your facilitator directs you there. Do not download it from any other source.
  2. Sign in with your school account. Use the email address your facilitator provides. This connects you to Optima Academy's organization inside the platform, which is what lets your facilitator see your completed work.
  3. Try the practice space first. Before your first graded activity, spend fifteen to twenty minutes in an open practice session to get comfortable with movement and with the tools described below. The first time you use any of them should not be during graded work.
🔑 TERMS YOU WILL SEE IN EVERY VR ACTIVITY

Every VR activity in this course is written using these six terms and no others, so you only need to learn them once.

Session
The shared space you enter to do the activity. Your facilitator posts a session link in the Canvas module; opening that link is how you get in.
Setting
The environment inside the session, for example a public square, a seminar room, or a gallery. For solo activities, choosing which setting to use is your decision, not something assigned to you. Pick whatever setting best fits what the activity asks you to do.
3D object
Anything you can place in the setting and position, such as a marker, a shape, or an image, to stand for an idea. Activities that ask you to represent an argument spatially will ask you to place and arrange 3D objects.
Shared whiteboard
A surface in the session that you or your group can write and draw on. Used for activities that ask you to record a short written answer inside the session itself.
Capture
A still image of the setting, taken from inside the session. Used when an activity asks you to submit a picture of the space you built or annotated.
Recording
A saved narration of your voice and movement inside the session, used when an activity asks you to present or explain something out loud rather than in writing.
📋 COMPLETING A SOLO VR ACTIVITY, STEP BY STEP
  1. Read the full activity instructions in Canvas before you put on the headset, or before you open the desktop version. Know what you are supposed to do before you enter the session.
  2. Open the VR platform and select your session link from the Canvas module.
  3. Join the session. If the activity asks you to choose a setting, choose one suited to the task, as described above.
  4. Do the numbered steps listed in the assignment: placing and annotating 3D objects, writing on the shared whiteboard, or whatever that activity specifically asks for.
  5. If the activity requires a capture, take one before you leave the session. It saves automatically to your account.
  6. If the activity requires a recording, record it clearly and at a normal pace. It also saves automatically.
  7. Leave the session when you are finished.
  8. Open Canvas, go to the assignment's submission page, and upload your capture or recording, following that assignment's specific submission instructions.
DO NOT HAVE A HEADSET? THERE IS ALWAYS A DESKTOP ROUTE
Every solo VR activity in this course is fully doable on the desktop version of the platform. If a headset is unavailable to you for any reason, use the desktop version and tell your facilitator you are doing so. If you expect to use the desktop version regularly, say so early, so your facilitator can confirm the activities work well for you that way.
🔧 COMMON PROBLEMS AND FIXES

I cannot find my session.

Check the session link in your Canvas assignment again and confirm you are signed into your Optima Academy school account, not a personal one. If the link still does not work, contact your facilitator; the session may need to be reopened on their end.

My recording is not saving.

Confirm you have a stable internet connection; recordings need one throughout. Try a shorter recording, around thirty seconds, and see whether that one saves. If it still does not, report the problem to technical support rather than repeating the attempt several more times.

I cannot find a 3D object I need.

Search the object library inside the platform first. If the exact object named in the assignment is not available to you, place a similar one instead and note the substitution on the shared whiteboard or in your submission, so your facilitator knows it was a stand-in.

The headset is uncomfortable.

Adjust the head strap and the lens spacing for your face before you start. Take a short break every twenty-five to thirty minutes. If you feel nauseated or disoriented at any point, remove the headset immediately and resume later, or switch to the desktop version for that session.

I do not have a VR headset at all.

Use the desktop version described above. Every solo VR activity in this course is built to work fully that way.
