❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
How much of who you are did you choose?
Last week ended with four incompatible accounts of human nature and no way to settle between them. This week takes a different approach: instead of asking what people are like before society, it asks what society does to people, and how. That question can actually be investigated.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS STILL WONDER
Last week wonder meant noticing that arrangements are arrangements. This week it gets harder and more personal. Miner's technique turns it on your own bathroom, and Lesson 2.02 turns it on your own sense of self. The discipline being built here is the willingness to look at what formed you without either resenting it or defending it, which is more difficult than it sounds and more useful than either reaction.
