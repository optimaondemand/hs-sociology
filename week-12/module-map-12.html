❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
Does specialization bind a society together, or does it just sort people into ranks?
Monday and Tuesday look at the division of labor from two angles: Smith's arithmetic of output and Durkheim's question of cohesion. Wednesday and Thursday turn to what specialization does to power and prestige, where Marx sees one axis and Weber insists there are three. Both halves of the week are really the same question asked twice: when a society sorts people into different roles, what actually holds the sorted people together, or apart?

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS COURAGE
This week asks you to look directly at inequality, who has wealth, who has honor, who has power, without flinching from an uncomfortable case and without excusing it with a tidy story. Marx and Weber disagree about almost everything else, but both refused to look away from stratification or pretend it was accidental. The journal prompt asks you to do the same with a real group: pick the honest, divergent case instead of the comfortable, easy one.
