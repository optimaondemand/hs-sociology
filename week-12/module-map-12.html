Week 12: Crime, Deviance, and American Institutions
❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What does a society need to stay orderly, and how much pressure to conform is too much?
Durkheim asks whether a society could ever eliminate crime, and argues that it could not and should not want to. Tocqueville turns the question around: a free society may tolerate too little dissent.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS COURAGE
Courage here means facing an uncomfortable social fact without flinching or explaining it away. Durkheim insists that crime cannot be wished out of existence. Tocqueville insists that a free society can punish honest disagreement without passing a single law.
