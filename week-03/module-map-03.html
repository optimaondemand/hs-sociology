Start here. This page shows you everything the week contains.
❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What is society made of, if it is not just people?
The first two weeks were preparation. This is the week sociology becomes a distinct discipline rather than a branch of philosophy, because this is the week it claims an object of study that belongs to nobody else. If that claim fails, there is no subject here. Both thinkers this week are arguing that it does not fail.

THE TEST YOU WILL USE ALL WEEK
Durkheim's three criteria. Every one has to hold.
External
Did it exist before me, and will it outlast me?
General
Does it hold across the group, not just for me?
Constraining
Does it push back when I resist it?

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS HONESTY
Honesty here means intellectual honesty: following evidence where it goes, including when it goes somewhere uncomfortable. Durkheim took the most private act he could think of and reported what the records actually showed rather than what he expected. Tocqueville admired America and then wrote several hundred pages on how a society like it could lose its liberty without noticing. Both are examples of the same discipline, and the wrap-up survey will ask you where you practised it this week.
