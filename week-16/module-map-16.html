Week 16: Community in Modern America and Capstone Preparation
❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What stands between a person and the state, and does the community you know still have it?
Tocqueville named the question, Durkheim gave it a mechanism, Tönnies a structure, Nisbet its political stakes, Putnam the evidence. This week the last modern thinkers in that line are read against Tocqueville’s own words, and the question turns toward the Community Flourishing Capstone.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS COURTESY
Courtesy: engaging a disagreement without contempt for the person making it. Nisbet’s pessimism and Berger and Neuhaus’s optimism each get a hearing on their merits. Tocqueville gives the same virtue an older sense, a shared humanity extended across strangers and rivals. The Capstone’s recommendations are written in that spirit.
