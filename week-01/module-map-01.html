❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
Are human beings naturally social, or do they have to be made social?
You will answer this twice: once in the intro survey before you read anything, and once at the end of the week. Nobody expects the two answers to match. The point is to find out what moved you.

🔄 WORK THAT RUNS ALL SEMESTER
1
Your Sociological Journal. Set up one Word document in OneDrive this week and keep every entry in it. By Week 16 it becomes the raw material for your capstone. Students who record real observations now save themselves a great deal of work later.
2
Classical Thinker Profile, due Week 8. You will write a short essay on one classical sociologist. Start noticing now which thinker you find yourself arguing with, because that is usually the one worth writing about.
3
Four projects across the semester. The Thinker Profile in Week 8, a Community Institution Study in Weeks 9 to 11, four short case studies in Weeks 12 to 15, and the Community Flourishing Capstone in Weeks 16 to 18. Together they are 30 percent of your grade.
💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS WONDER
Wonder is the willingness to find something strange that you have always taken for granted. It is the beginning of this whole discipline. Sociology started when people looked at arrangements that had seemed permanent and natural, watched them come apart, and realised they had been arrangements all along. You will practise the same move on your own life this week, in your journal, and again in the wrap-up survey.
