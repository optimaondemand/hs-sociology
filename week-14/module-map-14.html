Week 14: Media, Culture, Globalization, and Identity
❓ ESSENTIAL QUESTION
What does the system you are formed inside of do to you?
Two lessons on media and globalization, and two readings twenty-three centuries apart, ask the same question in different vocabularies: not what a culture teaches, but what it makes of the people raised inside it.

💡 VIRTUE · SELF-GOVERNMENT
Plato’s answer is that a person becomes what he practices imitating. Weber’s is that a discipline can outlive the belief that built it and keep shaping people who never held that belief. Self-government gets harder, not easier, when the constraint no longer announces itself as anyone’s choice.
