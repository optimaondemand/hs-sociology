❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
Does the thing that connects you to other people also change what "connected" means?
The city was the first technology to put strangers permanently within reach of one another, and Simmel spent a career asking what that did to the mind. This week the course applies the same question to the newest such technology: the screen. You finish Simmel's essay on the metropolis, then turn his method loose on communications technology, then read Simmel again on the senses themselves, a century before anyone had a reason to ask what a video call does to a face.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS SELF-GOVERNMENT
Self-government here means governing your own attention in an environment engineered to capture it. Simmel's blasé attitude is one kind of self-government: a deliberate, chosen filter rather than an involuntary breakdown. Choosing whether a technology is building Gemeinschaft or Gesellschaft in your own life, rather than simply accepting whatever it makes easiest, is another. The wrap-up survey will ask where you noticed yourself doing this, or failing to.
