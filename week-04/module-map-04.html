❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What holds a society of strangers together?
Week 3 established that social facts are real. This week asks what they add up to. Durkheim gives a structural answer that runs against most people's intuition, and Simmel, born in the same year and looking at the same Europe, asks an entirely different question and builds a different discipline out of it.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS STILL HONESTY
This week shows two versions of it. Durkheim reports that suicide rates climb in booms as well as slumps, which is awkward for every tidy explanation including the one his readers wanted. Simmel refuses to say whether the city is finally good or bad for us, and holds two true things that pull against each other. Reporting an inconvenient finding and declining to resolve a question are both forms of the same discipline, and most people find the second harder.
