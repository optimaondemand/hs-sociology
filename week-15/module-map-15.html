Week 15: Culture Wars, Character, and Moral Formation
❓ ESSENTIAL QUESTION
How does a society form character once it can no longer assume everyone agrees about the good?
Lesson 15.01 describes the structure of moral disagreement. Lesson 15.02 asks how any single person comes to have character at all. A French sociologist and a Greek philosopher stand behind the two questions.

💡 VIRTUE · COURTESY
Courtesy here means describing a genuine moral disagreement precisely and stating its strongest objection fairly, without telling the reader which side to take. That is a discipline, and it lets two people who disagree all the way down argue with each other rather than past each other.
