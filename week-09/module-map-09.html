❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
What have you learned to see that you could not see nine weeks ago?
This week does not add a new thinker. It asks you to hold the whole quarter together as one argument: what binds a person to the people around her, and what happens when that bond weakens. You will also step back from any single thinker's conclusions to ask a harder question underneath all of them, namely how any of them knew what they claimed to know.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS PERSEVERANCE
Perseverance is not only finishing a hard book. It is the discipline of holding nine weeks of difficult material together long enough to see the shape of it, instead of letting each week close and disappear the moment its quiz is submitted. Synthesis is harder than learning something new, because there is no fresh material to carry you forward. It is entirely your own effort to connect what you already have. This week asks for exactly that effort, and the Community Institution Study will ask for it again.
