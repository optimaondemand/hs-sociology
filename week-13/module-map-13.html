Week 13: Urbanization, Technology, and Social Relationships
❓ THE QUESTION THIS WEEK IS ABOUT
Does the thing that connects you to other people also change what “connected” means?
The city was the first technology to put strangers permanently within reach of one another, and Simmel asked what that did to the mind. The week applies the same question to communications technology.

💡 THE VIRTUE THIS WEEK IS SELF-GOVERNMENT
Self-government here means governing your own attention in an environment built to capture it. Simmel’s blasé attitude is a filter the metropolis produces in the mind without anyone choosing it. Self-government chooses instead: deciding whether a technology is building Gemeinschaft or Gesellschaft in your own life, rather than accepting whatever it makes easiest.
